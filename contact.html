LOCATIONS COVERED

Regional & National Travel

Our primary studio is located in Chennai, TN, covering local properties. We regularly document architectural commissions across major regional hubs under standard travel rates.

Primary Zone (Chennai)

No travel surcharge applies within city limits. Local bookings can accommodate split shoot blocks over two days easily.

South India Regional Zone

Covers Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kochi, Mysore, and Goa. Travel surcharges are limited to actual transport.

National Travel Zone

Covers Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Jaipur. Shoots in these zones require scheduling coordination at least 4 weeks in advance.
