USAGE TERMS

Commercial Usage & Image Licensing

All photography images delivered are subject to standard copyright guidelines. A clear understanding of rights ensures that both the creative teams and the client groups are protected legally.

Personal & Portfolio Use

Images may be used by hiring designers, architects, or staging professionals to showcase their design capability on portfolios, personal websites, and social media feeds.

Marketing & Commercial Use

For real estate developers, hotels, or rental properties, images are licensed for marketing brochures, advertisements, booking websites, and print campaigns.

Third-Party Licensing

Should subcontractors (furniture makers, lighting companies, flooring providers) wish to use the images, they are required to purchase secondary licensing rights from Lumen.

Editorial PR & Publication

Image licenses accommodate editorial submissions to magazines like *Architectural Digest*, *Cereal*, or *Dezeen* with standard credit guidelines.
