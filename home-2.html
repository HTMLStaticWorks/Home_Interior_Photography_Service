THE METHODOLOGY

The Collaboration Process

01
Enquiry & Consultation
Submit your project details via our digital questionnaire. We assess project scales, floor plans, material styling objectives, and natural window alignments.
02
Pre-Production & Styling
We compile a visual shot list coordinating props, floral arrangements, key accent accessories, lighting directions, and precise shoot schedules.
03
The Photography Session
A careful, deliberate capture session combining soft ambient light with calculated flash exposures to preserve depth, texture, and realistic view balances.
04
Editorial Post-Production
Individual exposures are manually blended to correct color casts, align perspectives, clean distractions, and optimize lighting balances.
05
High-Res Delivery
Images are uploaded to a private client proofing gallery for selection. Final high-resolution print-ready web webps are transferred digitally with standard licensing.
