RESIDENTIAL INTERIOR

The Courtyard Residence

Capturing Brutalist Scale & Delicate Warmth

Designed by Mira Kothari of *Kothari Design Atelier*, The Courtyard Residence in Chennai is a study in raw brutalist materials softened by warm wood and central landscaped courtyards. The primary architectural objective was to construct an open-air transition between active spaces while keeping indoor temperatures balanced.

Our photography approach required a multi-pass lighting method. By aligning the shoot times with Chennai's morning sun path, we captured soft, direct shadows falling on the textured concrete walls. To balance the brightness of the courtyard with the rich wood of the interior dining table, we combined low ambient flash layers with long exposure shots, avoiding harsh artificial over-exposure.

Shoot Highlights

  • Shot with specialized wide tilt-shift lenses to preserve horizontal concrete planes.
  • Coordinated twilight sessions mapping artificial lighting values from the kitchen.
  • Styled accents using stone and clay earthenware to match the concrete textures.
