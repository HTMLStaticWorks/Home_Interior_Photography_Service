SERVICE SPECIFICS

Residential Interior Photography

Capturing the designer's intent, material textures, and spatial flow.

Residential interior photography is not just about showing the layout of a room; it's about translating three-dimensional design selections into flat editorial frames. We cooperate closely with stylists, architects, and luxury builders to ensure every element—from custom drapery folds to grain structures in walnut paneling—is documented with precision.

Our studio uses high-resolution medium format systems combined with specialized tilt-shift lenses to prevent spatial distortions. We avoid using broad artificial light boxes, relying instead on ambient natural windows, adding subtle flash increments only to balance shadows and maintain realistic color parameters.

Creative Shoot Deliverables

Spatial Overall Exposures

Wide, balanced views displaying complete rooms, kitchen islands, or living layouts mapping spatial connections.

Vignette & Detail Focus

Close-up frames emphasizing hardware selections, fabric pairings, custom carpentry joinery, or styled side tables.

Natural Lighting Transitions

Capturing shadows falling across surfaces during key solar windows to give interior spaces a soft, realistic atmosphere.

Color Retouched Files

Manually blended ambient/flash exposures delivered in high-resolution, print-ready formats with standard licensing rights.

Booking Parameters

We recommend scheduling residential interior shoots at least 3-4 weeks in advance to coordinate styling sheets and window alignments.

  • Avg. Duration: 4 - 8 Hours
  • Retouching style: Multi-pass blending
  • Lead Time: 10 Business Days
  • Licensing: Portfolio + Social Media
